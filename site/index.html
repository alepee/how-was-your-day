ONE MONDAY

A day, from the first idea
to “how was it?”

Follow one ordinary day: a thought on the way to work, a check-in with Camille and Sam about a beta launch, and the evening when you try to remember what was decided.

  1. 08:42Voice memo

    An idea on the way in.

    Say it before it is gone. Press ⌃⌥⌘R from any app and recording starts, without bringing a window forward. The menu bar icon shows it is running and lets you pause or end it.

    The shortcut is configurable in Settings and needs no Accessibility permission.

    ⌃⌥⌘RFrom any app. Again to stop.
  2. 09:58Before the meeting

    The meeting is about to start.

    Your calendar knows. A reminder offers to join the call and record, or to record without joining. Starting by hand? Name it, pick a type and the language. Before a session with other people, Start waits until you declare everyone present informed and agreeing: the stamp keeps the time, and so does the session.

  3. 10:00Recording

    Be in the room, not in your notes.

    Your microphone and your Mac’s audio are recorded on two synchronized tracks. Jot a few words when something matters; each note is timestamped. Pause when the discussion goes off the record: nothing is captured until you resume.

    Your AirPods just connected. HWYD does not follow silently: it asks whether to switch, and warns that a headset microphone lowers what you hear. Unplug a microphone and recording continues on the default input.

    Stopped too early? Resume recording picks the session up again for 24 hours, notes and all.

  4. 10:04Transcript

    Who said what.

    The transcript is written on your Mac by Apple Speech, in the language spoken on each track. Each detected voice gets a tile: click it to hear only that voice, then name it, confirm a suggestion, or ignore the echo of your own speakers.

    A voice is remembered only when you choose to, person by person. Every suggestion waits for your confirmation, and a voice correction can be undone.

  5. 10:06Summary

    So, what did we decide?

    The summary lays out the overview, decisions, next steps with their owner and due date, then the details by topic. Each point carries the time it was said: one click plays the passage, so you can check before you quote it.

    You choose who writes it. A local model server such as LM Studio, a hosted provider with your own API key (Mistral, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), or your own coding agent: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and others. An agent can add what it knows about the people and projects, in a separate Context section.

    Summaries are written in English or French, following the language you choose for HWYD. Turn on automatic summaries and one starts as soon as the transcript is done and every voice is named.

  6. 10:15Sharing

    Pass it on.

    Export the summary as PDF or Markdown and pick the sections. Context notes from your agent start unticked, since they may hold private information.

    Your own tools can read every session too: versioned Markdown and JSON exports, and a standalone MCP server for your agents.

  7. 18:30Evening

    How was your day?

    Everything is in one place: the coffee with Léa, the idea from this morning, the check-in, the 1:1 with Sam. Search titles and what was said, filter by type, and open any session right where it matters.

BUILT TO STAY CLOSE

Your Mac.
Your context.

Audio stays on your Mac

Recording, transcription and voice separation run locally. For summaries, you decide where the transcript goes: a local server, your own agent, or a hosted provider you enable with your key. Audio, notes and voice prints are never sent.

Recording is your call

Every recording starts with an explicit action and a visible state. Your declaration that the people present agree is kept with its time, in the session and its exports. Voice memory is a separate, opt-in choice for each person, and you can see, withdraw or erase what each session added.

Yours to keep, or to delete

Export Markdown and JSON, or let your agents read sessions through MCP. Voice vectors never enter exports. Deleting a session deletes its audio.
