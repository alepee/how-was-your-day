# How Was Your Day v0.17.0: Welcome tour, split voices, images in notes

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.17.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.17.0)

A first launch now opens a welcome tour that sets HWYD up step by step ([#52](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/52)). A voice shared by two people can be split in the transcript ([#47](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/47)), images can be pasted into the capture notes ([#50](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/50)), and session kinds come down to three, with a simpler consent step ([#49](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/49)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **A welcome tour on first launch**, which sets up calendars, models, your agent and its context folder
- **Split a voice** that was in fact two people, or give a passage a voice of its own
- **Paste images into the capture notes**, with a caption, exported with the session
- **Three session kinds**: memo, exchange and meeting
- **Consent in two steps** on the start button, instead of a switch before a disabled button

## ✨ What's New

### Welcome tour ([#52](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/52))

On the first launch, when the library is empty, a tour opens over the app. Six steps present recording, calendars, transcription, voice separation, summaries by your agent and the agent's context folder. Each offers its setup:

- allow calendar access, which turns meeting reminders on the first time, and pick the calendars to follow;
- install the transcription model of the Mac's language, then the voice model;
- connect an installed agent in one click, installing the Claude Code or Codex ACP adapter with npm when it is missing. This also turns on automatic summaries for sessions recorded from then on;
- choose the folder where the agent keeps your context.

Each step has an animated banner, which stands still with Reduce Motion. Users who already have sessions are not interrupted. The tour opens again from Settings › General or the Help menu.

Calendar exclusions now start empty instead of « RH, entretien, médical »: no meeting is left out of reminders until you add keywords in Settings › Calendar. This applies to anyone who never edited them.

### Split a voice ([#47](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/47))

Diarization sometimes gives one voice to two people who speak one after the other. A transcript passage's menu now offers **Split the voice from here…**: a sheet shows how many passages fall on each side of the cut, with a way to listen to them, and lets you choose which part keeps the current name. The other part becomes a new voice and its name editor opens. **New voice…** gives a single passage a voice of its own.

Both can be undone from their notification and never touch a voice fingerprint. A fingerprint already memorized from a voice that held two people is not cleaned by the split; [`docs/voice-memory.md`](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/blob/main/docs/voice-memory.md) explains how to withdraw it.

### Images in the capture notes ([#50](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/50))

During a capture, paste an image with Cmd+V (capture screen or detached notepad), add one with the **Image** button next to **Timestamp**, or drop it. Each image is a timed note entry with an editable caption and a remove button, and shows in the review.

Images are stored as PNG without metadata, at most 4096 px, next to the session's data, never in SQLite. Markdown exports copy them to `attachments/<id>/` and link them; JSON note entries carry an `image` key only when they hold one, so existing exports are unchanged. Search and `hwyd-mcp` name the image without a link.

### Three session kinds, consent in two steps ([#49](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/49))

Session kinds are now **Memo** (`voice-memo`, alone), **Exchange** (`exchange`, with others) and **Meeting** (`meeting`). Calls, conversations and free sessions recorded before become exchanges, in the app, its filter, `hwyd-mcp` and the next export of each session. A new session is an exchange by default. Recording a calendar meeting preselects **Meeting**, and the kind can be changed while recording.

For an exchange or a meeting, the start button now works in two steps: it first reads **Consent obtained** and lands the dated stamp, then becomes **Start Recording**. The menu bar follows the same two steps, without the stamp. The declaration and its time are recorded as before.

## 🐛 Fixes

- Settings › General › Updates showed the placeholder version 0.4.0 in development builds. It now shows the same version as the sidebar ([#51](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/51)).

## 🚧 Known limits

- Images are added during a capture only; the review can caption or remove them. They are not sent to the summarizer.
- Image links resolve from the flat `session-*.md` export, not from the copies under `.hwyd/generations/`.
- Files already exported keep their old session kind (`call`, `conversation`, `ad-hoc`) until the session is exported again.
- The global shortcut and meeting reminders still start a recording without the consent declaration.

## 📦 Installation

Use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu, or:

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized).

No migration needed. Older session kinds are read as exchanges without rewriting the data.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- The language set in HWYD and in macOS when the interface or a summary is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
