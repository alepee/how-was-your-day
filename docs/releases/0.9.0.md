# How Was Your Day v0.9.0: Simpler, reversible voice identity

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.9.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.9.0)

Voice identity is simpler to use and every step can be reversed ([#4](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/4)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **New session screen**: nothing is captured before you press the button
- **Stacked notifications** with action buttons
- **Voice identity on the tiles**, with two inferred channels
- **Undo and split** for every voice correction, and per-session withdrawal of voice samples

## ✨ What's New

### New session screen

**Nouvelle session** opens a setup screen: choose the session type, optionally a name, then start recording.

### Notifications

App messages are stacked notifications in the corner of the window. Information leaves after a few seconds; a notification that asks for a decision carries its buttons (Réessayer, Réglages, Afficher) and stays until answered.

### Voice identity on the tiles

The « Identité des voix » panel is gone. Use **Mémoriser cette voix…** (with confirmation) for a named voice, **Nommer et mémoriser…** otherwise. Recognition runs again on the session after each enrollment.

### Two inferred channels

No more channel to choose: a voice print has two channels, microphone and call, deduced from the track. A person known on only one of them can be suggested on the other, above a stricter threshold (0.85 instead of 0.75).

### Reversible corrections

Name, regroup, link and ignore show **Annuler** in their notification. A merged voice can be split back with **Séparer** in the tile menu.

### Traceable samples

Each voice sample remembers its session. In Settings › Audio et voix, open a person to see what each session added and **Retirer** it, **Effacer l’empreinte** without blocking the person, **Effacer et opposer**, or **Lever l’exclusion** (a new enrollment is then required).

## 🚧 Known limits

- Voice matching thresholds are not calibrated on a representative corpus.

## 📦 Installation

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later).

This build is signed with an `Apple Development` identity but is not notarized by Apple. On first launch, if macOS blocks it, use System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Open Anyway**.

Former « Proche » and « Salle » samples are merged into the microphone channel automatically. Samples stored before 0.9 have no known session; they are listed together and can be withdrawn as a group.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- Your audio setup (built-in microphone, USB interface, Bluetooth headset) when recording is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
