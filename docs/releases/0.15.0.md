# How Was Your Day v0.15.0: Switch microphones, resume a session, choose what to export

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.15.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.15.0)

A recording no longer stops when your audio setup changes: you choose the microphone, HWYD asks before following a new one, and changing the output device keeps the session going ([#41](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/41)). A session you stopped or that was interrupted can be resumed for 24 hours ([#39](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/39)). Exporting a summary now asks which sections to include ([#40](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/40)), and sessions have a right-click menu ([#38](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/38)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **Choose the microphone**, before and during a recording, and decide whether to follow AirPods when they connect
- **Changing the output device no longer stops the session**
- **Resume recording** in a session that ended, up to 24 hours later
- **Choose the sections of a summary export**, with private context notes left out unless you tick them
- **Right-click a session** to open it, summarize it or delete it

## ✨ What's New

### Choose and switch the microphone ([#41](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/41))

The setup screen and the capture screen have a microphone picker. HWYD remembers your last choice and uses the Mac's default input when that microphone is not connected.

When macOS switches its default input during a recording (AirPods connecting, for example), HWYD no longer follows silently: a notification asks **Switch** or **Keep …**, and the same question shows in the capture screen. Not answering keeps the current microphone. For a Bluetooth headset, it warns that using its microphone switches it to call mode, which lowers the sound you hear. Permission to show notifications is asked the first time there is one to show.

If the microphone in use is unplugged, recording continues on the default input and HWYD tells you. With no microphone left, the session stops as interrupted and can be resumed. Changing only the audio output device no longer stops the session.

JSON exports and `hwyd-mcp` give the session's first microphone and each change.

### Resume recording ([#39](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/39))

**Resume recording**, in a session's header and its right-click menu, records the rest of a session you stopped or that was interrupted, up to 24 hours after it ended. The new audio follows the earlier audio, new notes follow the earlier ones, and the transcript marks **Resumed at …** where recording started again.

When recording stops, the whole session is transcribed again. Corrections made to its transcript are lost, so you are asked to confirm first. An existing summary is kept and marked as made from an earlier transcript. JSON exports and `hwyd-mcp` give where and when recording resumed, in `capture_boundaries`.

### Choose the sections of a summary export ([#40](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/40))

Exporting a summary as PDF or Markdown first opens a sheet listing the sections the summary has. The overview is always included. Context notes start unticked on every export, since they come from a coding agent's own sources and can be private; when ticked, their sources are exported with them. The PDF counts only the exported sections. Nothing is remembered between exports.

### Right-click a session ([#38](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/38))

In the library and among the recent sessions of the sidebar, right-clicking a session opens it, generates or regenerates its summary without opening it (or cancels a summary running or waiting), and deletes it. Deleting is unavailable while work runs or waits on the session.

## 🚧 Known limits

- Microphone switching has been tested with simulated devices only. Its behaviour with AirPods, Bluetooth headsets and USB interfaces, and the length of the gap while the capture switches, are still to be confirmed on real hardware.
- When the output device changes, the capture rebuilds its system audio stream, which leaves a short gap in that track.
- Resuming has been checked with synthetic audio. Echo reduction restarts at each resume or microphone change and has not been measured on real recordings yet.
- Voices are recognized in one pass over the whole session: a voice recorded on two microphones may be split in two, and can be merged in voice review.

## 📦 Installation

Use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu, or:

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized).

No migration needed. Until you pick a microphone, HWYD records the Mac's default input, as before.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- Your audio setup (built-in microphone, USB interface, Bluetooth headset) when recording is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
