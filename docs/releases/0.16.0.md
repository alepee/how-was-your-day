# How Was Your Day v0.16.0: English summaries, declared consent

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.16.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.16.0)

HWYD now writes in English as well as French: one setting sets the interface, summaries and their exports ([#43](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/43)). Before recording other people, the new session screen asks you to declare them informed and agreeing, and the session keeps that declaration with its time ([#44](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/44)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **Choose HWYD's language**: interface, summaries, exports and new voice labels, in French or English
- **Declare consent before recording**: Start stays disabled until the people present are declared informed and agreeing
- **A redesigned new session screen**, as a single card with its own actions

## ✨ What's New

### Choose HWYD's language ([#43](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/43))

Settings › General › **HWYD language** offers System language, Français and English. The choice sets the interface, summaries and their Markdown and PDF exports, labels given to new voices ("Voice 3", "Voice to name") and the examples. It applies at the next launch; Settings offer to relaunch, except during a capture or processing. By default HWYD follows the macOS language, as before.

English summaries use English prompts at every stage, with a local model, a hosted provider or a coding agent, and record their language. Summaries already written keep theirs. The transcription language stays that of each session.

### Declare consent before recording ([#44](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/44))

The new session screen is now a centred card: the name as its title, the kind as chips, microphone and language as menus, and its own actions. For any kind but a voice memo, **Start** stays disabled until you turn on **People present informed and agreeing**. Doing so lands a dated stamp on the card. The declaration is never carried over from one session to the next. The menu bar applies the same rule.

The session records the declaration and its time, and the JSON export carries it as `consent_announced_at`. HWYD records your declaration, it does not collect consent from the participants, and the declaration is not consent to voice enrollment.

## 🚧 Known limits

- The benchmark suite measures French summaries only; English summary quality is not measured yet.
- Session exports, the MCP protocol and validation reasons sent to models are not translated.
- The global shortcut and meeting reminders still start a recording without the consent declaration. The capture screen checkbox stays available to declare it afterwards.

## 📦 Installation

Use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu, or:

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized).

No migration needed. Sessions recorded before this version have no declaration time (`consent_announced_at` is null).

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- The language set in HWYD and in macOS when the interface or a summary is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
