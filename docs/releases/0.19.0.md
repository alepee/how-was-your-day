# How Was Your Day v0.19.0: Your notes in summaries, your voice during calls

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.19.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.19.0)

A summary written by your agent now starts from your notes and pasted images ([#63](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/63), [#65](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/65)), and the Mac's built-in microphone keeps your voice at its usual level during a call ([#74](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/74)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **Summaries start from your notes**: timed notes, free notes and pasted images go to your agent with the transcript
- **The built-in microphone works during calls**: no more near-silent microphone track, and joining or leaving a call no longer stops the recording
- **Change the type of a finished session**: memo, exchange or meeting, without recording again
- **Name a person who opposed recognition by hand**, without any voice print

## ✨ What's New

### Your notes in agent summaries ([#63](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/63), [#65](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/65))

A summary written by your agent now starts from your notes. Timed notes, free notes and the images pasted during the session (up to eight, when the agent reads images) go with the transcript, each timed note tied to the passage under way when it was taken. The agent writes with them in mind (who speaks, the right names, what you found important) without copying them, and the transcript prevails when a note says otherwise: the contradiction is pointed out in the context section.

The summary export then warns that your notes were used, so you can check that nothing you wanted to keep private shows through. Hosted providers still receive no notes.

### Change the type of a finished session ([#66](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/66))

The label above a finished session's title (**Memo**, **Exchange**, **Meeting**) now changes its type, without resuming the recording. A voice memo turned into a meeting does not gain a consent declaration: its export still records that none was made.

### Naming a person who opposed recognition ([#73](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/73))

A person who opposed voice recognition can still be chosen by hand to name a voice, from **Known People** in the voice menu or through the MCP tool `rename_speaker`, and `get_participants` lists them. Opposition keeps blocking their voice print, enrollment and recognition; naming reads and writes no print.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### The built-in microphone during calls ([#74](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/74))

While a call app uses the Mac's built-in microphone (FaceTime, Google Meet in Chrome, Safari or Firefox), macOS gives other apps only the microphone's raw capsules, about 35 dB quieter. The microphone track was nearly silent, and joining or leaving a call stopped the recording with « La capture a perdu des buffers audio ». HWYD now averages and raises the capsules, whether the call was already running when the recording started or begins later, and starts again on the new format when a call starts or ends, leaving a gap of a fraction of a second out of the recording. External microphones are unchanged: the restart that keeps their level when a call starts stays. [`docs/capture-diagnostics.md`](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/blob/main/docs/capture-diagnostics.md) has the measurements.

### Next steps without empty offers ([#68](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/68))

Next steps no longer list an offer the other person declined, a joke, or a reply to a suggestion made outside the recording, such as in the call chat. When in doubt about whether a commitment is serious, the summary leaves it out. Existing summaries are unchanged until generated again. The summary agent now receives its instructions in English whatever the summary language, which the prompt states once; the summary keeps the language you chose.

### Agents found from your shell's `PATH` ([#67](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/67))

The welcome tour now finds Claude Code, Codex and their npm adapter when their directory is added to the `PATH` in `~/.zshrc` (Claude Code's own installer, nvm, fnm, Volta), so **Install and Connect** shows as soon as the `claude` command is installed. When npm cannot write to its global folder, the adapter is installed under `~/.local` instead, and when npm is missing the message asks for Node.js.

## 🚧 Known limits

- The microphone gain during calls is fixed, measured on one MacBook Pro: the level may differ on other Macs, and room noise and the call heard from the speakers come up with it.
- The final version of the call fix was checked with synthetic tests; the measurements behind it come from real FaceTime and Meet calls on its first version.

## 📦 Installation

Use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu, or:

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized).

No migration needed. Existing sessions are read unchanged.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- Your audio setup (built-in microphone, USB interface, Bluetooth headset) when recording is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
