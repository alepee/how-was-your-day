Voice memory is now available directly from the speaker editor.

- Choose **Nommer et mémoriser** to name a speaker and save a local voice fingerprint, or **Nommer seulement** to edit the transcript alone.
- New diarized transcripts automatically look for remembered voices and show name suggestions to confirm.
- Microphone enrollment uses echo-reduced audio. Close microphone, room and remote-call examples stay separate.
- The voice panel shows enrolled channels and supports forgetting people.

Includes the responsiveness improvements and persistent waveform cache from 0.1.1.

Update with `brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day`.

Voice recognition remains experimental and needs at least 20 seconds of clean speech. Fingerprints stay in the local macOS keychain. This release remains locally signed and not notarized by Apple.
