# How Was Your Day v0.13.0: In-app updates and summary export

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.13.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.13.0)

HWYD now updates itself, a summary can leave the app as a PDF or Markdown file, and sessions open on their summary ([#20](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/20), [#21](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/21)). This release also fixes a microphone track recorded far too quietly when a meeting app opens the microphone after the recording started ([#19](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/19)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **In-app updates**: **Check for Updates…** in the app menu and the menu bar
- **Summary export** as PDF or Markdown
- **Summary first**: a session opens on its summary when one exists
- **Microphone level kept** when a meeting app joins after the recording started

## ✨ What's New

### In-app updates ([#18](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/18))

**Check for Updates…** is in the app menu and the menu bar, and Settings › General has an Updates section. Automatic checks stay off until you accept the prompt Sparkle shows on the second launch, and no system profile is sent. Scheduled checks are skipped during a capture or processing, and a relaunch waits until both end; a check you ask for always runs. Homebrew users keep updating the same way: the cask now declares that the app updates itself.

### Summary export

The summary tab has an **Export summary** menu:

- **PDF**: follows the summary layout on A4 pages, with the overview, key points, decisions, next steps with their owner and due date, and every topic expanded with its passages.
- **Markdown**: the title, date and confirmed participants, then the summary.

Context notes a coding agent draws from its own sources stay out of both exports, since they can hold private information unrelated to the session.

### Summary first

Session tabs are ordered Summary, Transcript, Notes. A session opens on its summary when one exists, otherwise on its transcript.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Microphone recorded about 30 dB too low ([#19](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/19))

When a recording started before the meeting app opened the microphone (typically with **Join and Record**), the microphone track could be captured about 30 dB too low until a pause and resume: the meeting app's voice processing reconfigures the input device under the running stream. HWYD now notices when another application starts reading audio input during a capture and restarts its microphone stream once, after a short settle delay. The restart gap is left out of the timeline, like a pause.

## 🚧 Known limits

- The microphone restart has been checked on synthetic captures and on a recorded session, not yet end to end during a live meeting. Dictation or Siri can trigger a restart, which costs a few milliseconds of audio.
- In-app updates start with this version: 0.12.1 and earlier must be updated once through Homebrew or the DMG.
- Exported summaries stay in French, like the summary.

## 📦 Installation

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized). Later versions can be installed with **Check for Updates…**.

No migration needed.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- Your audio setup (built-in microphone, USB interface, Bluetooth headset) when recording is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
