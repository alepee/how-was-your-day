# How Was Your Day v0.18.0: Tidier voices, forgotten recordings, queued work

[![Downloads for this release](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/alepee/how-was-your-day/v0.18.0/total.svg)](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/releases/v0.18.0)

Voices are now tidied up after each transcription: echoes, duplicates and fragments no longer ask for a name, and a voice that seems to hold two people is flagged ([#60](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/60)). A recording left running stops on its own ([#56](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/56)), and work asked during a transcription waits its turn instead of being refused ([#58](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/58)).

## 🎯 Highlights

- **Fewer voices to sort out**: probable echoes are hidden, duplicates merged, fragments set aside
- **A voice that seems to hold two people is flagged**, with the passages to listen to before deciding
- **A forgotten recording stops on its own** after 5 minutes without speech, once it has asked
- **Work waits its turn** during a transcription, and erasing a voice print runs at once
- **Release notes in the update prompt**, instead of the GitHub page

## ✨ What's New

### Voices tidied up after transcription ([#60](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/60))

Voice separation works one track at a time, without knowing how many people there are. After each transcription with voice separation, HWYD now corrects its usual mistakes:

- **Probable echo.** A microphone voice that is only the call picked up by the microphone is hidden, and its tile says **probable echo**. **Restore** brings it back.
- **Merged voices.** Voices of the same track that are one person are merged; the tile says **merged**, and its menu offers **Separate … (merged automatically)**.
- **Too brief.** A voice too brief to name keeps its passages in the transcript under "Other voice". It is not counted among the voices to name and does not hold back the automatic summary. **Restore**, or naming it, counts it again.
- **Two people in one voice.** When a voice seems to hold two people, a line under the tiles says so. **Listen to these passages** plays the passages concerned, then **Separate** moves them to another voice, or **One person** dismisses the question. Nothing moves until you answer.

Every one of these decisions can be undone from the tiles or from its notification, and none reads or writes a voice print. [`docs/voice-post-processing.md`](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/blob/main/docs/voice-post-processing.md) describes each step and how its thresholds were measured.

Two related changes:

- Recognizing voices no longer reads the audio again after a transcription: it reuses what voice separation computed. A brief voice is recognized from 20 seconds of speech spread over the session.
- Memorizing a voice samples its speech across the whole session instead of its first minute. A voice that mixed two people could otherwise be memorized from the wrong person's passages.

### A forgotten recording stops on its own ([#56](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/56))

When nobody has spoken for 5 minutes, on the microphone or in the call audio, HWYD asks **Are you done?** in the capture screen and as a notification, with **Not yet** and **Stop**. Without an answer, the recording stops 2 minutes later, keeps its audio and notes, and a notification says so. Speech coming back withdraws the question.

Speech is detected on the Mac by the macOS sound classifier, so a fan, typing or music without voices does not keep a recording alive. Nothing it hears is stored. It can be turned off in Settings › Audio & Voice.

### Work waits its turn ([#58](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/58))

Asking for work while a transcription runs no longer greys out the controls or refuses the request. Memorizing a voice, recognizing voices, a summary or a model install starts after the work in progress, in the order asked. A notice says so, and the voice tiles show **Waiting…** with a cancel action. A waiting enrollment reads the voice again when it starts: if it was renamed meanwhile or the person opposed recognition, nothing is memorized.

Erasing a voice print, withdrawing a session from it, opposing recognition or lifting an opposition now runs at once, even during a transcription. It cancels the person's enrollments still to come, and a transcription that read the print before it was erased no longer proposes that person.

## 🐛 Fixes

- The update prompt showed the GitHub release page, with its header and menus, in its small notes pane. It now shows the release notes text, with a link to the full page ([#54](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/pull/54)). This applies from this release on, whatever version is installed.

## 🚧 Known limits

- Sessions already transcribed keep their voices as they are until they are transcribed again.
- The flag for two people in one voice is the least proven rule: its thresholds rest on two confirmed cases from one meeting. A second person who only speaks in passages of a few seconds is not flagged, and a session recorded at a very low level never is.
- Short fragments of a person named elsewhere in the session can end under "Other voice" instead of that name.
- A flagged voice holds back the automatic summary until you answer, like an unnamed voice.
- The thresholds were measured on one user's sessions, in French.

## 📦 Installation

Use **Check for Updates…** in the app menu, or:

```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask alepee/tap/how-was-your-day
```

Or download `HowWasYourDay.dmg` below (Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later; signed and notarized).

No migration needed. Existing sessions are read unchanged.

## 🐛 Bug Reports

If you encounter issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/alepee/how-was-your-day/issues/new/choose) with:
- HWYD and macOS versions
- Your audio setup (built-in microphone, USB interface, Bluetooth headset) when recording is involved
- The exact error message

Do not attach recordings, transcripts or summaries of real conversations.
